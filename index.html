How it works

  1. Select text in any app
  2. Press the hotkey from Recording and ask your question out loud
  3. The answer appears where the recording indicator is. Press the hotkey again to ask a follow-up; the cross closes the window

Click an answer to copy it

In terminals the selection isn't read: a copy shortcut would interrupt the running program there
