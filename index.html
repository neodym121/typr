How it works

  1. Select text in any app, or press the Quick Ask hotkey to ask about anything
  2. Press the dictation hotkey from Settings → Hotkeys and ask out loud, or click the pencil and type
  3. The answer appears where the recording indicator is, under your question. Ask again for a follow-up; the cross closes the window
